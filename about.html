About Project Interlock

Opportunity grows when the right connections lock into place.

We are building a regional ecosystem where Black and Latino students can see themselves in STEM, gain future-ready skills, and access the people and opportunities that move careers forward.

Our Mission

Close the opportunity gap.

Project Interlock connects students to affordable education, scholarships, mentors, internships, and employers—creating a continuous bridge from exploration to employment.

Our Vision

Build shared prosperity.

We envision Greater Boston as a national model for inclusive AI and STEM talent development, with institutions working together rather than in isolation.

Why now

The AI economy is accelerating. Access must accelerate with it.

Greater Boston launch

Local depth. Scalable impact.

Our first phase focuses on communities within roughly 20 miles of Boston.

We will partner with Massachusetts community colleges—where eligible students can access free tuition—and public universities with engineering, computer science, data, and technology programs.
