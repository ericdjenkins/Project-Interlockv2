Who’s building this

Eric Jenkins is relaunching Project Interlock.

Originally founded by Dr. David E. Wharton.

This relaunch is dedicated to the memory of Dr. David E. Wharton and Clyde Myron Neville III, a Project Interlock alumnus.

Project Interlock began at North Shore Community College as a science and engineering mentoring program directed by Dr. David Eugene Wharton—known to many students as “Doc Wharton.” He built a pathway that helped Black and Latino community-college students transfer into university engineering programs and persist to graduation.

Dr. Wharton, who passed away in 2024, was an educator, counselor, and advocate for equal opportunity. His work in Massachusetts—including teaching at NSCC and earlier leadership in workforce education—created the model this Greater Boston AI and STEM relaunch honors.

Clyde Myron Neville III was a Project Interlock alumnus whose career and mentoring showed what that pathway can make possible. This relaunch carries his example forward for the next generation of students.

Eric Jenkins is bringing that connected pathway forward. He brings more than 25 years of technology leadership—building engineering teams across the U.S. and India—and nearly 30 years of coaching and mentoring young people. He was recognized as MetroWest YMCA Volunteer of the Year for longstanding service to youth.

Founding partnership packages

Concrete ways to commit.

These are suggested starting points from our partnership prospectus and can be combined. Recognition and benefits will be documented in each agreement. Nothing is announced as a partnership until it is in writing.

Access partner

$10,000+

Transportation, supplies, childcare, emergency microgrants, and family events that keep students in the pathway.

Learning partner

$25,000+

Curriculum, instructors, software licenses, robotics kits, laptops, and labs for AI and engineering learning.

Experience partner

$50,000+ or 10 paid placements

Internships, research, capstones, mentors, and site access that turn classroom skill into workplace identity.

Pipeline partner

$100,000+

Fund a cohort, completion supports, paid experiences, and evaluation for the 2027 launch year.

Institutional partner

Defined in-kind commitment

Recruitment, advising, space, faculty, transfer mapping, and permitted outcome data from a college or university.

EDU

Education partners

You provide

Admissions and transfer maps, advising, faculty time, labs, and student support.

Students get

A clear public-college route into engineering, computer science, and related credentials.

We coordinate

Cohort recruitment, wraparound supports, and employer connections around shared milestones.

CO

Employer partners

You provide

Two or more mentors, paid internships or project seats, speakers, and site visits.

Students get

Experience, references, professional identity, and a hiring pathway.

We coordinate

Matching, safeguarding, supervisor feedback, and evaluation.

COM

Community partners

You provide

Trusted outreach, family engagement, program space, and wraparound referrals.

Students get

Belonging, culturally responsive delivery, and a warm handoff into college and work.

We coordinate

Enrollment, mentoring, and career exposure so constituents are not navigating systems alone.

FND

Funding partners

You provide

Flexible capital for delivery, completion supports, paid experiences, staffing, and evaluation.

Students get

The non-tuition supports that decide whether free college becomes a career.

We coordinate

Program delivery, reporting, and a shared scorecard on persistence, internships, and jobs.
