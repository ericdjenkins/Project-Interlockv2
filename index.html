Our Mission

Building a connected pathway from potential to profession.

We close the opportunity gap by surrounding students with the resources and relationships that turn STEM interest into lasting careers.

Working with community colleges, state universities, employers, and community organizations throughout Greater Boston, Project Interlock helps students move confidently from exploration to education—and from education to employment.
