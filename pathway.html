The Interlock Model

No single program closes an opportunity gap. A connected pathway can.

Each stage creates momentum for the next, with trusted adults, measurable milestones, and warm handoffs between partners.

01

Discover

See a future in STEM.

Community outreach, career exposure, role models, and hands-on experiences help students identify interests and possibilities.

02

Learn

Build knowledge and credentials.

Students enter affordable college, certification, or training pathways with academic and financial support.

03

Prepare

Develop confidence and networks.

Mentors, projects, professional skills, and portfolio development prepare students for opportunity.

04

Experience

Apply skills in the workplace.

Paid internships and employer projects create experience, references, and professional identity.

05

Launch

Enter and advance in a career.

Job matching, interview support, alumni networks, and continued coaching turn a first role into long-term mobility.

How success is measured

Progress that students and partners can see.

EnrollmentEducation and credential entry
PersistenceTerm-to-term continuation
ExperiencePaid internship placement
